Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before your dog's first visit. Can't find it? Call 07903 555424.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our daycare centre holds a 5★ licence, and our home boarding is licensed by Rushmoor Borough Council (Licence No. 25/00868/AWRHOM). We're insured with Petplan Sanctuary, and our team are DBS checked.

How do I book?

Call us on 07903 555424, send us a message through this website, or fill in our online booking form. Every new client starts with a free introductory meeting and assessment. For dog training, call 07714 944241.

Which areas do you cover?

Day care covers Ash, Aldershot, Farnborough, North Camp, Mytchett, Camberley, Frimley, Farnham, Guildford, Fleet and Hartley Wintney. Dog walking covers Ash, Aldershot, North Camp, Farnborough and Mytchett.

When will my dog be picked up and dropped home?

For a full day we collect dogs around 9–10:30am and bring them home between 3:30 and 5pm.

Does my dog have to be neutered?

We don't accept females in season, and we only take entire males up to the age of 10–12 months.

I have a puppy. Can they come to daycare?

Yes, from 10 weeks old or once they're through their vaccinations. Puppies get plenty of supervision, downtime and structure.
