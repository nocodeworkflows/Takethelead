Two ways the new site could look.

Both use the Take The Lead logo, the brand red and the same serif lettering as the logo. They differ in mood and layout. Open each one on a laptop and on your phone, then tell us which feels more like Take The Lead, or what you'd take from each.

Option A

Red Slab

Confident and detailed. A big red welcome card, a scrolling strip of credentials, and tidy service cards that show prices up front.

Feels like
An established, professional business. Lots of proof: licence number, insurer, prices and FAQs on the homepage.
Main call to action
Book a free meet & greet.

Option B

Shapes & Woodland

Warmer and more playful. Each service has its own shape, the private woodland gets its own section, and the free trial day sits in a red house-shaped badge.

Feels like
A friendly local business with a sense of fun. Less text, bigger photos, more room to breathe.
Inspired by

Over to you

Questions to answer as you look

  1. Which first screen makes you want to book? The red card (A) or the photo with the house badge (B).
  2. Prices on the homepage, or not? A shows them. B leaves them for the service pages.
  3. Meet & greet or trial day? Pick the one first step we lead with everywhere.
  4. How playful? B's shapes are fun. Do they suit a vet-nurse-led business, or feel too young?
  5. Anything to steal from the other one? A winner can borrow sections from the runner-up.
