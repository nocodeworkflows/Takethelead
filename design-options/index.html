New website · Design directions

Seven ways the new site could look.

Every option is a homepage built with your logo, your real photos and your real services and prices. A and B are close cousins. C to F push further in different directions, so you can see how far the look can move. G is clean and modern, with a dog you can play with. Open each one on a laptop and on your phone, then tell us which feels most like Take The Lead, or what you'd take from each.

First round

A and B: the brand red, warm and polished

Both use the brand red and a serif like the one in the logo. They differ in mood and layout.

Option A

Red Slab

Confident and detailed. A big red welcome card, a scrolling strip of credentials, and tidy service cards that show prices up front.

Feels like
An established, professional business. Lots of proof: licence number, insurer, prices and FAQs on the homepage.
Main call to action
Book a free meet & greet.

Option B

Shapes & Woodland

Warmer and more playful. Each service has its own shape, the private woodland gets its own section, and the free trial day sits in a red house-shaped badge.

Feels like
A friendly local business with a sense of fun. Less text, bigger photos, more room to breathe.
Inspired by
Yardstick Dog Hotel (Nashville) for the shapes and big statements. High Tails (Canada) for the calm photo-and-text sections.

Second round

C to F: four different personalities

Each one changes the colours, the lettering and the tone of voice. The brand red stays in all four, but it does a different job in each.

Option C

Muddy Paws Outdoors

Rural and photo-led. A full-width field photo, forest green and oat colours, and small touches from an Ordnance Survey map, like the map key and contour lines.

Feels like
A proper outdoor dog business. Wellies, fields and happy muddy dogs. The red is used sparingly, like a red wellie.
Inspired by
The Mucky Pup (UK) for owning the muddy outdoors. Barkbus for letting real dog photos do the work.
Main call to action
Book a free meet & greet.

Option D

Clinic Calm

Premium and quiet. Lots of white space, elegant lettering and one red accent. It leads with Karina being a registered veterinary nurse, and walks through a day at day care hour by hour.

Inspired by
Small Door Vet for the calm, premium feel with one accent colour.
Main call to action

Third round

G: clean, modern and interactive

Red and black on white, with sections that move as you scroll, and a spaniel who watches your mouse. Best seen live on a laptop.

Option G

Clean Modern

Crisp and current, with an interactive star. A springer spaniel in the hero turns his head to follow your mouse. Click to throw him a treat and he catches it in slow motion, right where he sits. Further down, services, numbers and photos animate in as you scroll.

Feels like
A polished modern brand that's still fun. Visitors will play with the dog, and that's memorable.
Inspired by
Modern product websites: big tight headlines, a sticky picture that changes as you scroll the services, and text that lights up word by word.
Main call to action
Book a free meet & greet.
Colours

The dog footage is a stand-in so you can see the idea. A short shoot of one of your own dogs on a plain backdrop (a slow head turn left to right, plus a few treat catches) would let us build it exactly like this.

Over to you

Questions to answer as you look

  1. Which first screen makes you want to book? Pick your top two of the seven.
  2. Is the interactive dog right for us? G's spaniel is fun and memorable. Would you want a Take The Lead dog filmed the same way?
  3. What should people feel first? Professional and safe (A, D), outdoorsy (C), friendly and local (B, E) or bold and different (F).
  4. How much should the vet nursing lead? D builds the whole page around it. The others mention it alongside everything else.
  5. Prices on the homepage, or not? A, C, D and F show them. B and E keep them light.
  6. Meet & greet or trial day? B leads with a free trial day. The rest lead with the free meet & greet, as the current site does.
  7. Anything to steal from another option? The winner can borrow sections, like D's "day with us" or E's tear-off poster.
